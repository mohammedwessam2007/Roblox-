# Pre-Registered Pilot Protocol

STATUS: NOT RECRUITED. Requires explicit owner approval before any recruitment, contact, or session. RESULTS: NOT_RUN. No player data exists.

## Purpose and limits
Usability pilot only, N = 10-20. It can find confusion, crashes and friction. It cannot estimate retention, revenue, CTR or "which game is better" with statistical confidence. Do not present it as such.

## Design
Counterbalanced crossover: half of participants assigned AB (Flagship then Challenger), half BA. Assignment by pre-generated alternating list (seeded, recorded before session 1). Same device class within a participant. Fixed play window per game (e.g. 5 min; confirm before run).

## Outcome definitions (per participant, per game)
- assigned: participant placed in an order (AB/BA).
- started: facilitator confirms game launch attempted.
- loaded: player character controllable in arena.
- first_meaningful_action: first STEAL (Law) or first CUT POWER (Heist).
- second_choice: Law: THROW or PLANT after steal; Heist: REROUTE or OPEN ESCAPE after blackout.
- first_complete: first full round/heist finished (goal, fail, or timeout both count; record which).
- replay_offered: AGAIN prompt displayed.
- voluntary_replay: participant starts another round without facilitator prompt.
- preferred_game: Flagship, Challenger, or neither (neither is a valid, reportable answer).
- early_exit: leaves before 30 s of play.

## Analysis (pre-specified)
Report counts and proportions per outcome with exact binomial (Clopper-Pearson) 95% CI: lower = Beta(0.025; x, n-x+1), upper = Beta(0.975; x+1, n-x); x=0 lower=0, x=n upper=1. Order effects reported descriptively (AB vs BA). No significance claims, no multiple-comparison-based winners. Qualitative notes listed verbatim by category.

## Consent, age, privacy
- Owner approval required first. Participants 16+ only unless a parent/guardian is involved per owner/legal guidance; do not recruit minors without guardian consent and owner/legal review.
- Written (or recorded) informed consent; voluntary; may stop anytime; no payment beyond what owner approves.
- Record pseudonymous ID only (P01...). No names, Roblox usernames, faces, voices, or emails in the CSV. Screen recordings only with separate consent and stored privately.
- Retain raw notes max 90 days (owner may change); delete on request.
- Costs: $0 budget; no incentives without owner approval.

## Stop rules
Stop session on distress, motion discomfort, or any report of photosensitivity symptoms; log as an incident.
