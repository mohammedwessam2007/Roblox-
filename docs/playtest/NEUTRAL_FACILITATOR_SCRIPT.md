# Neutral Facilitator Script

STATUS: NOT USED. Do not run without owner approval and participant consent.

Rules: read verbatim; do not praise, hint, point, or explain mechanics unless safety or a hard blocker (after 60 s stuck, say: "Take your time, what are you trying to do?"). Do not say which game is yours or newer. Do not react to success or failure.

1. Intro: "Thanks for helping. We are testing the games, not you. You can stop at any time. I will not help during play unless something is broken."
2. Consent check: confirm consent given; confirm age eligibility; confirm no recording unless separately agreed.
3. Game 1 (per assignment): "Please play this for about [N] minutes however you like. Think aloud if you want."
4. Observe silently; record timestamps per outcome in PLAYTEST_OBSERVATION.csv.
5. After game 1: "What was happening in your own words? Was anything confusing?"
6. Game 2: same wording as Game 1.
7. Close: "Which did you prefer: the first, the second, or neither?" Record exactly as said. "If you wanted to play again, which and why?"
8. Debrief: thank, confirm data handling, offer deletion.
